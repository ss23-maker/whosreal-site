Who’s Real? Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Who’s Real? game for Android (package name com.sportspheremedia.whosreal) handles your information. The game is published on Google Play by Sportsphere Media Pty Ltd, a company registered in Australia (“we” or “us”).

Summary

We do not collect or store personal information about you ourselves. You can play without an account, and your progress stays on your phone. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google uses some device information to show them, as explained below.

What the game stores on your device

To save your place, the game keeps the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your progress, such as stars, flag cards, badges, level, daily streak and Second Looks
  • a case you are partway through, so you can continue it later
  • your settings, such as sound and light or dark theme

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your phone’s settings deletes it.

Ads in the Android app

  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Most are optional: you choose to watch one for a reward, like taking back a send-home or keeping your streak. Sometimes a full-screen ad plays between cases.
  • To show ads, Google’s ad software uses your device’s advertising ID and basic device information, such as phone model, country and IP address. It uses these to pick ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Google explains how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the app’s settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • The web version of the game shows no ads.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It never changes a case because of ads. Every case plays the same for everyone.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools of its own.
  • It does not use your phone’s location services, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

In-app purchases

The Android app sells optional one-time extras through Google Play: Remove ads +, case packs and All Access. Google Play handles the whole payment, so we never see your card or payment details. When the app starts, it asks Google Play which extras your Google account owns so it can unlock them. That check happens between Google Play and your device, and we do not store it anywhere else.

Sharing your results

When you tap a share button, the game makes a result picture and a caption on your device. You can send them through your phone’s share menu, save the picture or copy the caption. You choose where they go, and they do not pass through us. A challenge link only includes the case, a shuffle number, your stars and your score. It contains nothing that identifies you.

Children

Who’s Real? is made for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads, updates and purchases in the game. Information Google collects when you use Google Play is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
